We collect phone numbers provided by customers for the purpose of sending order shipping notifications.

How We Use Your Information

Phone numbers are used solely to send transactional SMS notifications regarding the status of your order shipment, including pickup confirmation, transit updates, and delivery confirmation.

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are only used for order-related notifications.

Opt-Out

You may opt out of receiving SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, you will no longer receive shipping notifications.
